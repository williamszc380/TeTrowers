Special Blocks

How to Play

Drop blocks onto the platform and build the tallest tower you can. Your score is the best height your tower reaches.

Placement modes

Assisted (default)
Steps between the spots where your block sits flush with the block it will land on, and in half-block steps over empty space. While it falls it follows its target, so a support that shifts a little cannot make it clip a corner and miss.
Fixed
Half-block steps. No help while falling.
Manual
Pixel-precise. A tap moves one pixel, holding slides faster and faster, and dragging is 1:1.

Turns and lives

  • Each round deals 3 blocks. Place them in any order; the next round comes when all of them are down.
  • A turn runs from one drop to the next. The first block that falls off the tower during a turn costs a life. Any more losses in the same turn are free.
  • Blocks that are swallowed, blown up, melted or destroyed by spikes do not cost lives.
  • You have 3 lives. When the last one is gone, the run is over.

Milestones

Every 10 m of best height gives you a life back. A Crystal Heart restores one too, or gives you a shield when your lives are full. The dashed line on the playfield marks the next milestone.

The higher, the meaner

Good blocks (cold colours: white, green, blue) get rarer and bad blocks (warm colours: red, orange, black) more common as your tower grows. Every run ends eventually, so make every drop count.

The Good / Bad odds on screen are the chances for each block you are dealt at your best height. Tap them (or press O) to see every block's odds and which block unlocks next.

Petrify

Blocks that sit more than 14 m below the top of the tower slowly turn to stone (they look a little faded) and never move again. The base of a tall tower becomes solid ground.

Seeds

Every run has a seed. The same seed with the same special-block set deals the same blocks, so you can challenge friends on a seed, or everyone on the Daily Challenge.
